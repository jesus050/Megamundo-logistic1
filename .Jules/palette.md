## 2026-10-22 - Missing Accessible Names on Icon-Only Action Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only action buttons (like close modal '×' or remove item '✕') in this app frequently lack accessible names, making them difficult for screen reader users to identify.
**Action:** Always ensure that buttons consisting only of visual icons or characters have an `aria-label` (and optionally a `title`) that clearly describes their function.
