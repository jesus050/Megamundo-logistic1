## 2026-10-28 - Adding aria-label to icon buttons
**Learning:** Found some icon-only buttons in the user interface, specifically 'close camera' in the scanner, and 'remove product row' buttons in PedidosTrait. These don't have aria-labels, making them inaccessible for screen readers.
**Action:** Add aria-label attributes to all icon-only buttons like these so screen readers can interpret their meaning.
